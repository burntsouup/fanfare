Fanfare Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Fanfare does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No ads and no third-party tracking.

Your settings (which reactions are on, their hotkeys, and display preferences) are saved in a file on your own computer and never leave it.

The Microsoft Store version of Fanfare makes no network connections. The version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for app updates. That check sends only what any web request sends (such as your IP address) to GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
